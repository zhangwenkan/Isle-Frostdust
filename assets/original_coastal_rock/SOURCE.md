# Coastal rock sources

The five rock modules, eighteen shoreline placements, both UV channels and fitted contact bake are authored in Blender through Blender MCP. They use new explicit profiles, not Wandering Sword geometry or UVs.

The base palette, face washes and selected structural strokes are generated by `tools/paint_original_coastal_rock_planes.py`. `tools/stylize_original_coast_surface.py` adds filtered, reduced-palette mineral fields from **Rock Boulder Cracked**, by Dimitrios Savva (photography) and Dario Barresi (processing), Poly Haven.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_cracked
- License: CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license
- License statement retrieved 2026-11-10 and saved in `sources/polyhaven-license.html`.
- Original downloaded diffuse and metadata retained in `sources/`.
- Processing removes fine grains, limits the palette, rearranges broad fields, and merges them at low contrast. No photograph normal or displacement map is used.

The Sea of Stars screenshot is a visual composition/color reference only; no pixels are copied. No Wandering Sword model or texture is used in this new coastal asset. These are script-authored and CC0-derived textures, not claimed to be wholly hand-painted or wholly from-scratch artwork.


## Continuous coast integration (2026-11-11)

`integrated_coast_paint.png` reuses the original/CC0-derived `warm_rock_sides.png` palette and strokes, arranged continuously along the authored coast by `tools/paint_integrated_coast.py`. Its alpha channel is a material blend mask, not surface transparency. Existing grass paintings are sampled only at the top junction. The model `integrated_coastal_mass.blend` contains the original relief reconnected by a new continuous Blender-authored mass; it does not include Wandering Sword geometry. `integrated_coast_contact.png` is newly baked on this exact assembled geometry, with a dedicated second UV channel.
